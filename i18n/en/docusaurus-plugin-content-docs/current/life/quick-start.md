# Quick Start

Get up and running with Life in three minutes: sign in, log your first entry, and start understanding your data.

## 1. Sign In

1. Visit [life.ccleeai.com](https://life.ccleeai.com) (web, works on mobile and desktop browsers)
2. Enter your phone number and request an SMS code
3. Enter the code to sign in

<InfoBox variant="warning" title="Limited Beta">

Life is currently invitation-only: your phone number must be on the invite list to sign in. Public registration is not open yet. See the [FAQ](../faq).

</InfoBox>

## 2. Log Your First Entry

In the home input box, just say it like a text message:

- "lunch cost me 32 today"
- "took a 25 taxi home yesterday, paid with Alipay"
- "got paid, salary 12,000"

AI turns your sentence into a structured card (amount, category, date, account). Check it, adjust anything, and confirm.

<InfoBox variant="info" title="Made a Mistake?">

- Any single record can be edited or deleted later
- If AI misunderstood you, use a correction to teach it how you talk — it remembers, and gets better over time

</InfoBox>

## 3. Teach It Your Habits

- Add your **accounts** in settings (WeChat, Alipay, bank cards), then "transferred 500 from my bank card" links to the right account automatically
- Common phrasings can be taught: see [Feature Guide · Corrections](../feature-guide)

## 4. Query & Stats

- Just ask: "how much did I spend on food this month?", "what did I buy last week?"
- Or open the **stats page** for spending structure, trends, and your mood curve

## 5. Try Chat Mode

The home input is in **task mode** by default (logging and queries). Switch to **chat mode** for open conversation about spending, mood, or medication — answers are grounded in your real records.

See [Feature Guide · Two Modes](../feature-guide).
